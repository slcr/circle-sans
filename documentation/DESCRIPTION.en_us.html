Circle Sans is a modern geometric sans serif family, inspired by the type-characteristics of scandinavian architects and designers in the early 20th century.
The Circle Sans family includes nine weights (Thin to Black) in two widths (Normal and Narrow).

Based on Albert Sans by the Danish type designer Andreas Rasmussen (a.Foundry), with a redrawn Q and a small set of alternates frozen in as defaults.

Circle Sans is maintained at https://github.com/slcr/circle-sans. It is a fork of Albert Sans.
