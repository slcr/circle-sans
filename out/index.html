What changed04
Q

A new tail

The centred vertical bar read as the Quake logo. It is now a diagonal stroke crossing the bowl, held at a fixed −52° so the angle never drifts with weight.

g

Long stem by default

Previously tucked away in ss02. The accented forms — ğ ĝ ġ ģ — follow automatically.

&

Open ampersand

The “et” form, previously ss04, is now the default and keeps its own width and kerning.

ff

A tucked ff

New in v1.4, drawn after the fi: first arm trimmed, one fused crossbar. Until now the two f were kerned apart to hide the clash.

At Coffee Circle, we offer the world’s finest coffee and equipment. We source directly from coffee growers and pay high prices for high-quality coffee, and we treat people how we want to be treated. Since 2016 we have roasted in our own roastery in Berlin-Wedding, where experienced roasters bring every variety to perfection. We serve and ship freshly every day to deliver the best flavour experience, and we make the big world of specialty coffee easy for you: a broad portfolio for every way of brewing, a hand-picked selection of equipment, and a knowledge base and coffee finder to help you choose.

We promote positive social change in coffee-growing regions, support research to make coffee farming more climate-smart and secure the income of coffee farmers and their families for generations to come. Through the Coffee Circle Foundation we invest one euro per kilogram of coffee sold in projects for clean drinking water, sanitation and sustainable farming, all designed to carry on long after we have left. Since 2010 that has added up to several million euros.
